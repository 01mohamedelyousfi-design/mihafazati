# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Moroccan qualifying-secondary philosophy teachers (الثانوي التأهيلي، مادة الفلسفة) preparing and maintaining their Professional-Cumulative Portfolio (الملف المهني التراكمي). They work in school offices and staff rooms, often in short focused sessions between classes, on laptops and phones. They are not tech specialists; they need obvious structure, Arabic-first labels, and zero ambiguity about where a document belongs. A secondary audience: inspectors (المفتشون) who review portfolio completeness during evaluation visits.

## Product Purpose

A login-gated personal archive that serves as a living database for a teacher's educational files. Every uploaded document finds its exact place inside the official three-part taxonomy from the ministry framework:

- القسم التربوي الإداري: التشريع المدرسي، المسار المهني، دفتر النصوص
- القسم الديداكتيكي: تخطيط التعلمات، تدبير التعلمات، تقويم التعلمات
- القسم التكويني المهني: الممارسة المهنية، محاور التكوين، الصحة المهنية

Each section decomposes into محاور (axes) → عناصر (elements) → مستندات (documents), with levels الجذع المشترك / الأولى / الثانية where applicable. Success looks like: a teacher can file any document in under a minute, find any document in seconds during an inspection, and export/share the complete portfolio in ministry-accepted formats (Office, PDF, images, video). Login requires personal email, cycle (السلك), subject (المادة), and subscription code (رمز الاشتراك). Includes dashboard (لوحة القيادة), settings (notifications, privacy, storage/data), and a personal data-responsibility agreement before first use.

## Brand Personality

Institutional, calm, trustworthy. A well-kept national archive rather than a government office: warm paper-like surfaces, scholarly restraint, quiet confidence. It should feel like the digital equivalent of a beautifully organized personal library. Three words: رصين، هادئ، موثوق (dignified, serene, dependable).

## Anti-references

- Bureaucratic portal look: walls of stacked links, dated banner headers, cluttered tables, everything-shout-nothing-hierarchy.
- SaaS dashboard cliché: gradient heroes, identical stat-card rows, dark-blue-everything.
- Social feed styling: likes, comment threads, engagement noise. This is serious archival work.

## Design Principles

1. **Taxonomy is the interface.** The ministry's three-section tree is not hidden behind search or tags; it is the primary navigation. A place for every document, visible at all times.
2. **Filing must be faster than folder-digging.** Upload flows resolve their destination automatically from context; never make the teacher re-navigate to upload.
3. **Inspection-ready at any moment.** Completeness states (what exists, what's missing per axis/element) are always legible so a portfolio is never caught incomplete.
4. **Calm density.** Show real information depth without visual noise; whitespace and type hierarchy carry the load, not boxes and borders.
5. **Arabic-first typography as craft.** RTL layout, proper Arabic webfonts, numerals and dates in Arabic convention; French/Latin fragments render correctly inline.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA: contrast ≥ 4.5:1 body text, full keyboard navigation with visible focus, reduced-motion support via media query, semantic landmarks and Arabic `lang="ar"` / `dir="rtl"` throughout. Touch targets ≥ 44px for phone use between classes.
